About this website

This website marks the beginning of a tribute project dedicated to the PlayStation 2. More content will be added soon. Although its release took longer than expected, I wanted to publish it before the end of 2025, in reference to the 25th anniversary of this iconic console, released in March 2000.

This project also aims to recreate, on the front-end side, the PlayStation 2 menu visible when the console is in standby mode. It is a personal visual and interactive re-creation.

If you are interested, feel free to contact me to discuss it and to follow me on my social networks. You can also visit my GitHub to star this project or to check out my other repositories.

On this website, you will find general information about the console, its history, a selection of iconic games, as well as an overview of how it was received by the public and the press.

Announced in 1999 and released in 2000, the PlayStation 2 remains the best-selling home console of all time, with over 155 million units sold and a library of more than 3,800 titles.
